About the Association

The Kialikmin Clan Association Incorporated speaks for Min peoples across Telefomin District, North Fly District, Tunap Anstein LLG and Ngalummin in West Papua. We are one people. We share the same mountains, rivers, language families and ancestral memory.

Telefolip, the sacred origin site of Min peoples, lies in Telefomin District. The Association is currently pursuing a Provincial Land Court Appeal at Kiunga. The appeal concerns the rights of the people whose land underpins one of the world's largest open-pit mines.

26%+

More than a quarter of Papua New Guinea's total national export earnings come from Min peoples' ancestral territory, through Ok Tedi Mining Ltd at Mt. Fubilan.

K0 has been received from any LLG, DDA, Provincial Government or mining company toward this advocacy in seven years.

Become a Member

Membership is open to Min peoples as Ordinary or Associate members. Both Papua New Guinea and West Papua (Indonesia) citizenship are recognised.

Members are registered by the clan chief for your place. Contact your local chief, or ask the Secretary-General to put you in touch with one.

1. Find your chief

Each place has a chief appointed by the Association.

2. Give your details

Your chief records your name, village, citizenship and membership type.

3. Stand together

Join the united voice of Min peoples across provincial and national borders.

Support the Appeal Fund
